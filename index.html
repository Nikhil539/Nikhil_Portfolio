JobTrack AI / an agentic workflow for job applications

JobTrack AI turns a noisy inbox into a structured application pipeline. It identifies job-related Gmail messages, asks an LLM for schema-constrained details such as company, role, status, interview dates, and deadlines, then validates the response before updating the tracker.

The workflow is designed around reliability: emails are matched against existing applications in a defined order, invalid model responses are recorded instead of silently changing data, and repeated syncs avoid duplicate processing.

Explore JobTrack AI ↗

  3. 03

  4. 04

    Match applications and update a visible, auditable status history.
